# Short rater guidance

Use the included approved rubric v1.1 unchanged. Score each blinded output
independently against its source; do not rank A against B.

Distinguish material diagnostic details from minor metadata. A detail is material
when it affects the issue's meaning, triggering conditions, reproduction, observed
or expected behavior, or a source-supported constraint. Minor metadata may be
incidental labels, identifiers or administrative details; judge its role in context
rather than treating any field as automatically important or unimportant.

Penalize an omission only when the relevant rubric criterion justifies it. Explain
what was omitted and how it affects that criterion, citing source and output
evidence. Do not apply a blanket deduction for every missing detail or transfer a
fidelity concern automatically to unrelated criteria. These are interpretation
reminders, not additional criteria or changes to the 1?5 anchors.

Follow rubric v1.1 for inherited ambiguity, missing source details, uncertainty
and unassessable judgments. Do not invent information or use automatic scores.
